Database Design

Entity Relationship Design

The database design shows the relationship between faculty members and their qualifications.

Faculty

  • faculty_id (PK)
  • name
  • department_id (FK)
  • designation
  • specialization
  • joining_date
  • experience_years
1 ─── ∞

HAS QUALIFICATIONS

Qualification

  • qualification_id (PK)
  • faculty_id (FK)
  • degree
  • specialisation
  • university
  • year_awarded

Database Design Decision

A faculty member can have multiple qualifications. Therefore, Faculty and Qualification have a one-to-many relationship. The faculty ID is stored as a foreign key in the Qualification table.
